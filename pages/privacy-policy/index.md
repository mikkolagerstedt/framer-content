## Privacy Policy

### Who are WE?

My name is Mikko Lagerstedt a fine art photographer from Finland. I'm the owner of the website: www.mikkolagerstedt.com.

### What information do we collect from you?

### 1. Tutorials & Free Presets

If you subscribe to my blog or opt-in in my tutorials and to receive the free Fine Art Lightroom Presets you subscribe to our newsletter, we require just an email address and first name. We do not require other information. If you leave a comment on this site, which may require your email address, that information is not used in any way.

### 2. Mikko Lagerstedt Customers

Once you receive the products or services you have a chance to opt-in to my newsletter and update information about the products. For your account we only use your email address and first name. No other information is stored.

---

### How to unsubscribe

At the bottom of every email you receive from us is an unsubscribe button. This will automatically unsubscribe you from all future communications. If you opt into multiple mailing lists, you have to manually opt-out of each email list.
