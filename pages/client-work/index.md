### **Nikon “Human Prompt”**

Nikon invited me as one of five image-makers to participate in the "Human Prompt" series. Rather than feeding a prompt into an AI tool, we each interpreted the same creative brief in our own way. I ventured into Lapland’s untouched landscapes, capturing its serene magic with my signature atmospheric style. The project followed our journeys and documented our distinct approaches, resulting in a diverse collection of visuals and stories that celebrate human creativity.

### **Nikon Z 8 Launch**

For the launch of the Nikon Z 8 camera. I was invited as one of the creators to work with Nikon and capture the natural beauty with the Nikon Z 8.

The Movement is an eight-part limited series following nine image-making creatives on their journey together as they use the all-new Nikon Z 8 for the first time.
