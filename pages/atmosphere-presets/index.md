# Atmosphere Preset Collection

Atmosphere preset collection is a visionary new preset collection to simplify your editing process. The collection includes the latest fine art presets by Mikko Lagerstedt. If you enjoy the look of Mikko's photography, look no further. Uplift your photographs with this unique moody preset collection.

The collection includes 86 presets, including color palettes, overall presets, and effects. The selection consists of 2 varieties of the intensity of the overall presets to suit your photography.

* 48 overall presets with two versions each to suit your photographs
* 20 color palettes
* 18 effects presets
* For Lightroom CC Classic (7.3 and later) & Camera Raw ACR 9/10/11
