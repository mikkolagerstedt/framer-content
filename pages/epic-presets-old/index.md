## Frequently Asked Questions

### How to install and use the presets?

The EPIC System comes with an installation and how-to-use tutorial, so you don’t have to worry about it.

### Which version of Lightroom or Camera RAW do I need?

The system works on Adobe Lightroom CC Classic 7.3 and later, Adobe Lightroom CC 3.4 and later, and Camera Raw ACR 9 and later.

### Can I use these presets on my phone?

The set includes twenty mobile phones presets, which you can easily download and apply for your photographs. You can also create your favorite presets if you import them individually from a DNG-file exported with the preset on your computer. Please find out how to do it [here](https://www.beart-presets.com/how-to-install-lightroom-mobile-dng-files).
