# What paper should you choose for your limited edition print?

You can choose three paper options from the print shop: Matte, Eco, and Pearl fine art papers. Each paper in the store is perfect for Mikko's photographs. The most popular print choice is the Matte option, which is versatile and ideal for most walls with its anti-reflective surface.

### Matte – Hahnemühle Photo Rag

The paper is ideal for challenging lighting conditions of its anti-reflective surface. Whichever print you choose will look great on this paper. The photo rag's beautiful, smooth surface and feel make this paper very versatile.

### Pearl – Hahnemühle Fine Art Baryta

The Baryta paper has a reflective surface and is ideal for a place with controlled light sources. It is excellent for star photography prints. Fine Art Baryta paper has a high color depth and image definition. Using barium sulfate in the premium inkjet coating ensures a glossy finish.

### Eco – Hahnemühle Bamboo

The bamboo print has a warmer base tone and will give you a smooth and anti-reflective surface. Bamboo represents spirituality and natural and resource-saving paper production.
