## About the creator

Mikko Lagerstedt is a self-taught fine art photographer from Finland. His work has reached millions of people and built a following of over 1.7 million on social media. Known for his cinematic, emotional style, Mikko creates images that feel like memories.

He released his first Lightroom preset collection in 2012. Since then, he has developed one of the only multi-phase preset systems built to guide photographers from the first tones to the final mood. His presets are used worldwide by both beginners and professionals.
