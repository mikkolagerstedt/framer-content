I was honored to be featured in one of the largest newspapers in the world [The Telegraph](http://www.telegraph.co.uk/travel/destinations/europe/finland/11148911/Night-II-nocturnal-photography-from-Finland-by-Mikko-Lagerstedt.html?frame=3065916). It's a great reminder that I'm on the path that I should be and it inspires me to work more for my photography.

My project Night II was also featured on the [TwistedSifters](http://twistedsifter.com/2014/10/finland-night-photography-by-mikko-lagerstedt/).
