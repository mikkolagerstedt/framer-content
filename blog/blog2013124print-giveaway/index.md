I'm really happy and overwhelmed by all the people following my work on various pages, for example [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/pages/Photography-Mikko-Lagerstedt/137616549627247?v=wall), [Google+](https://plus.google.com/+MikkoLagerstedt/posts) and now on my [tumblr](http://mikkolagerstedt.tumblr.com/) site. The print giveaway will be valid through 4th of December to 17th of December 2013.  
You will be contacted via Facebook and I will make a post of the winner also on my page after the timeline.
