A beautiful foggy morning from last summer. I shot this after a really long night photographing trip to Hanko. I remember I was really freezing here, because of the humidity and after a long night out.

### Equipment

Nikon D800, Nikon 16 - 35 mm f/4.0 VR and Sirui Tripod R-4203L & Sirui Ball Head K-40X  
ISO 50, 23 mm, f/6.3 and 1/10 sec.

I used my [Lightroom Presets](/presets) to edit the colors here.
