Hey everyone. I recently got few questions about Winter photography so I decided to make a list of things to consider while going out to photograph in cold weather. Remember to check the [Night Photography tutorial](http://www.mikkolagerstedt.com/blog/2013/11/5/top-5-tips-to-photograph-stars-night-sky) which I made earlier for more information about my approach to photography.

### 1. Equipment

This is not really about equipment, but how to handle your gear when you go out to take pictures in cold environment. Very simple, yet sometimes forgettable thing to do is to recharge your Camera batteries. And what I have experienced is to have couple of extra batteries with you in a warm inside pocket of your jacket. This will help your batteries to stay charged if you are planning to go out when temperature is - 10**°**C (14ºF) or colder. I usually have one battery in camera and two in the jacket.

### 2. Planning

If you haven't decided on a location it's fine, but what I have found out is if you don't have a plan on where you are going, you might not get the shot you want. Now of course there are plenty of times I have not had any plan and I have gotten a photo I really like. But usually by scouting a location and planning the timing you will have lot's more chances to get a decent image. I mainly use Google Maps for my location scouting and with a [SunCalc](http://www.suncalc.net/) -web tool you can relatively easy predict where the sun will be at what time. Which will help you choose a location time and even plan on the composition. IMGIMG?
