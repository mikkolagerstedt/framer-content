After a long wait, I finally present you the new fine art landscape and night sky [Lightroom Preset Collection Saga](/saga-presets)! If you want to add a unique look to your night and landscape photographs the Saga Presets are perfect for you! It includes over 180 presets to suit your style with my post-processing techniques. The collection uses the same technique of step by step process as the Phase Presets. Below you can find before and after photographs edited with the new collection.
