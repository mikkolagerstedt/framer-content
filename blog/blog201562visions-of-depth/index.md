I created new series of photographs, [Visions of Depth on Behance](https://www.behance.net/gallery/26063061/Visions-of-Depth). It was just released and featured in Behance. As you may have noticed, I have updated the website and will be adding all of these works to my print shop as well.

I'm currently working on my Masterclass Night Tutorial, and it should be ready to release in about a month. If you wish to be one of the first to review it, please leave a comment on this post and you may have a chance to get it before it's released.
