Quiet, cold and blue. It has been quite a change in the weather for the past two weeks. Cold and clear skies, now we just need this to continue so we can all enjoy it as much as possible! Here is a dual exposure vertorama taken at the coast of Finland merged together in Photoshop. Hope you like it.

Nikon D800, Nikon 16 - 35 f/4.0, Sirui Tripod R-4203L & Sirui Ball Head K-40X  
(Samyang 14 mm f/2.8 for the stars)

Sea: f/4.0, 120 sec, 16 mm, ISO 200  
Stars: f/2.8, 30 sec, 14 mm, ISO 3200
