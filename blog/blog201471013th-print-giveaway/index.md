I'm overwhelmed and truly inspired to have such a huge following in social media. So to show my appreciation, it's that time again - giveaway time! I will be giving away one 76 cm wide [Metal Print](http://www.redbubble.com/people/mikkolagerstedt/shop/metal-prints?ref=artist_shop_product_refinement) from one of my photographs! We have also updated the shop section on the site where you can purchase some of my work: [Buy Art](http://www.mikkolagerstedt.com/shop/)!

If you are interested in my fine art [Lightroom & Camera Raw presets](/presets), they are on sale until 10th of August!

### **How to participate in the giveaway?**

Just leave a comment in this blog post before **31st****July, 2014** and you are in the draw!  
**Update:** Winner will be announced on 6th of August.
