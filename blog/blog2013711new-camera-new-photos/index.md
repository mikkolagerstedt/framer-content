I hope to go out more and take pictures with this little beast and with my D800. If you have any questions drop them here.

Fist image I took, I used the "macro" -option and I feel that it worked out well.

The two night images were taken at the same night, just after sun had set. I didn't have a tripod for this camera so I had to lean on my D800's tripod to shoot these.

This camera is definitely great for street photography, even though I haven't got the time to shoot with seriously it yet on the street.
