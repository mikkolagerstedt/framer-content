Hi everyone. I get lots of emails about my work.  
  
This time I would love to see your work and give you my honest feedback. You can submit your Photography Portfolio feedback before Tuesday 24th, September. (Edited due to a huge number of entries)  
  
So how to enter? Simply follow these steps:

1. Create a new email  
2. Use Title: Portfolio Feedback - (Your name)   
3. Include in the message your website address or link to your work  
4. Use couple of words to describe your work  
5. Send the email to: mikko.lagerstedt@gmail.com

Note: It may vary how long it takes me to response on your email depending on the amount of emails I get, so be patient. :)
